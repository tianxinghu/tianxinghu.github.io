01About

I develop and evaluate machine learning methods for biological data, with an emphasis on representation learning, foundation models, and generalization.

I am particularly interested in how models capture biological information, how learned representations generalize across datasets and tasks, and how to build more reliable learning methods for single-cell and multi-omics data.

Before joining Emory, I earned an M.S. in Data Science from New Jersey Institute of Technology and a B.S. in Applied Statistics from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.
